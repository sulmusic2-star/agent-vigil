# Commercial hypothesis and proof gates

**State:** hypothesis, not valuation or revenue.

## Buyer and job

The likely buyer is an engineering platform, application-security, compliance,
or developer-productivity owner who must allow AI-generated changes without
letting the authoring agent grade its own work. The job is an enforceable,
searchable answer to: *which objective evidence supported this change at merge
time?*

## Packaging hypothesis

- **Open source:** CLI, Action, adapters, receipt schema, local policy.
- **Team hypothesis:** $29 per active PR author per month for hosted retention,
  policy sync, and repository rollout.
- **Business hypothesis:** $12,000-$30,000 per year for organization controls,
  approvals, analytics, and standard support.
- **Enterprise hypothesis:** $50,000-$150,000 per year for SSO, audit, SIEM,
  data controls, self-hosting, SLA, MSA, and DPA.

These ranges are pricing experiments. Current official comparables show paid
per-user code-review products and custom enterprise control planes:
[CodeRabbit](https://www.coderabbit.ai/pricing),
[Qodo](https://www.qodo.ai/pricing/), and
[LangSmith](https://www.langchain.com/pricing).

## One path to $1M ARR

A purely illustrative mix is 50 Business customers at $12,000 plus eight
Enterprise customers at $50,000. It is arithmetic, not a forecast. Agent Vigil
currently has no evidence for those customer counts, contract values, or
renewal rates.

## Sequence

1. Align npm, GitHub Releases, Marketplace, checksums, README, and Action pins.
2. Validate the centrally operated App in one disposable outside repository.
3. Earn 10 external repositories and 1,000 external receipts.
4. Publish sanitized, reproducible false-verdict and caught-failure cases.
5. Measure setup completion, receipt generation, false verdicts, and weekly
   retained repositories. Do not optimize stars alone.
6. Interview only users who voluntarily open issues or opt in; do not cold-poach.
7. Offer two written-only paid pilots after three organizations request central
   control.
8. Build paid organization controls only after those requests confirm the job.
9. Scale hosting after a renewal or expansion.

## Kill or narrow conditions

Reconsider the hosted company if, after a serious distribution cycle, external
repositories do not retain the check, users treat INCONCLUSIVE as intolerable,
agent vendors make equivalent signed evidence native and portable, or false
verdict review costs exceed the value of the gate.
